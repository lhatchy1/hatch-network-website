QUICK REFERENCE

  • Always check conditions before launching - river levels can change rapidly
  • Water temp below 10°C: wear a drysuit or thick wetsuit
  • Carry a throw bag, whistle, and knife on every run
  • Scout rapids you haven't run before, especially after high water
  • Never paddle alone - minimum group of 3 recommended
  • Check weather forecast for upstream areas - rain in the mountains means rising water downstream
